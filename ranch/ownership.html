How it goes, start to finish.

  • Pick a share

    Eighth, quarter, half or whole. If you are not sure, the estimator on the Ownership page will tell you roughly how long each one lasts for a household your size, and how much freezer you need.

  • Reserve it

    We raise our cattle to around 24 months before any of them are offered, so an animal is already grown by the time you see it. Send us an inquiry and we will tell you which are available. A deposit holds your share; the balance is due before the animal goes to the processor.

  • You own a specific animal

    You get a bill of sale for your share of one identified animal, and its ear tag number. From that point it is partly yours, and we are boarding and caring for it on your behalf.

  • Follow your animal

    For about a month before it goes, your animal is yours to follow: photos, updates, and an open invitation to come out and stand next to it. That is the point of doing it this way.

  • Choose your cuts

    A few weeks before the processing date we work through the cut sheet with you: steak thickness, roast sizes, how much goes to ground. The choices trade off against each other and we will walk you through it.

  • Pick it up

    Your beef comes back vacuum-sealed, labelled and frozen, usually about three weeks after the processing date, sometimes four. Pickup in Argyle, or we deliver in the Dallas – Fort Worth area for half and whole shares.

What you are buying, in plain terms.

A share here is an ownership interest in one specific live animal, recorded in your name before that animal is harvested. You are not buying meat by the pound, and you are not making an investment.

That is not a marketing position. It is how the law works for a ranch this size in Texas, and it is also a more accurate description of what actually happens. We board and care for your animal on your behalf, and arrange its processing for you.

A share carries no financial return, no resale value and no buy-back. It buys you an animal and the beef that comes off it.

  • Terms and disclosures

When you reserve a share, you are purchasing an ownership interest in one specific, identified live Black Angus animal raised at our ranch in Argyle, Texas. You are not buying meat, and you are not making an investment.

You will receive a bill of sale conveying your ownership interest before the animal is harvested, together with a written animal-share agreement authorising us to board and care for your animal and to arrange its processing on your behalf. Your share is sold under the animal share exemption in Section 433.0065 of the Texas Health and Safety Code and 25 Texas Administrative Code Section 221.14(d).

Your animal is processed at a licensed Texas custom-exempt facility. Because the animal is processed for its owners rather than for sale, it is not inspected, and the law requires every package to be marked “Not for Sale.”
