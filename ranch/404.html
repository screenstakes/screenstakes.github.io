  • Terms and disclosures

When you reserve a share, you are purchasing an ownership interest in one specific, identified live Black Angus animal raised at our ranch in Argyle, Texas. You are not buying meat, and you are not making an investment.

You will receive a bill of sale conveying your ownership interest before the animal is harvested, together with a written animal-share agreement authorising us to board and care for your animal and to arrange its processing on your behalf. Your share is sold under the animal share exemption in Section 433.0065 of the Texas Health and Safety Code and 25 Texas Administrative Code Section 221.14(d).

Your animal is processed at a licensed Texas custom-exempt facility. Because the animal is processed for its owners rather than for sale, it is not inspected, and the law requires every package to be marked “Not for Sale.”

THIS MEAT HAS NOT BEEN INSPECTED BY THE TEXAS DEPARTMENT OF STATE HEALTH SERVICES.

The Ranch Partnership is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by the Texas Department of State Health Services or the United States Department of Agriculture.
