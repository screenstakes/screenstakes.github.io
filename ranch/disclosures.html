Terms and Disclosures

What a share is, legally, and what it is not. This page exists because the distinction is real and because you are entitled to it in plain language before you spend money.

The agreement, in short.

When you reserve a share, you are purchasing an ownership interest in one specific, identified live Black Angus animal raised at our ranch in Argyle, Texas. You are not buying meat, and you are not making an investment.

You will receive a bill of sale conveying your ownership interest before the animal is harvested, together with a written animal-share agreement authorising us to board and care for your animal and to arrange its processing on your behalf. Your share is sold under the animal share exemption in Section 433.0065 of the Texas Health and Safety Code and 25 Texas Administrative Code Section 221.14(d).

Your animal is processed at a licensed Texas custom-exempt facility. Because the animal is processed for its owners rather than for sale, it is not inspected, and the law requires every package to be marked “Not for Sale.”

THIS MEAT HAS NOT BEEN INSPECTED BY THE TEXAS DEPARTMENT OF STATE HEALTH SERVICES.

Your beef is for your own household — you, your family, your nonpaying guests, and your household employees. By law it may not be resold, donated, or commercially redistributed.

A share carries no financial return, no profit participation, no appreciation, no resale value, and no buy-back. Shares are not transferable. A share is a purchase of food, not a security or an investment.

Why it is structured this way.

  • You buy an animal, not meat

    Beef from an animal processed at a custom-exempt facility cannot lawfully be sold. What can be sold is the live animal, or a share of it, before it is harvested. That is why the price is a flat price per share rather than a price per pound, and why every share must be reserved before the processing date rather than after.

  • Your share attaches to one animal

    Not to the herd. Texas requires that a share be tied to one particular identified animal and that the whole animal be fully designated before slaughter. You will be given the tag number of yours.

  • It is food, not a financial product

    A share pays no return, cannot be resold, is not transferable, and will not appreciate. We will not buy it back. If anyone ever offers you a livestock share on the basis that it will make you money, that is a different kind of transaction entirely and it is regulated accordingly.

  • If something happens to the animal

    Livestock is livestock. If an animal you hold a share in is lost or has to be pulled, we will move your share to another animal of comparable age and finish, or refund you in full. Your choice. We will tell you as soon as we know.

  • What you take home is for your household

    You, your family, your non-paying guests and your household employees. It cannot be resold or commercially redistributed, and every package will be marked accordingly.

A note on this page

This page describes the arrangement in ordinary language so that you can understand it. It is not a contract and it is not legal advice. The documents that govern a share are the written animal-share agreement and the bill of sale, both of which you receive before your animal is harvested, and both of which you should read.
