things every poker night needs

1. Agree on the buy-in first — Set one number everyone antes and stick to it. Mismatched stacks end friendships at 1am.
2. Blinds keep it moving — Small forced bets each hand stop everyone from folding forever. The game needs pressure.
3. One dealer, passed each hand — Rotating who deals keeps it fair and gives everyone a turn in the best position.
4. No phones at the table — Half the game is reading faces. Phones kill the tells and the vibe at the same time.
5. Cash out, don't add back — Endless rebuys turn a fun night into a money pit. Set a cutoff before you start.
6. Winner brings snacks next time — Takes the sting out of losing and keeps people coming back. That's how it becomes a tradition.
