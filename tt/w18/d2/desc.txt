what the numbers actually mean when you bet

1. Independent events don't build up — Coin flips, dice, and reels reset every single time. Nothing accumulates, nothing is owed to you.
2. Expected value is the only real number — Multiply what you win by how often, subtract what you lose by how often. Every casino game returns a negative number.
3. Variance is why anyone ever wins — Short-term swings hide the math. That's the entire product — the edge is real but slow enough to feel beatable.
4. Small edges compound brutally — A 2% edge over 1,000 bets isn't 2%. It's near-certainty, because volume converts probability into outcome.
5. More bets = closer to the math — One bet is a coin flip. Ten thousand bets is a spreadsheet. The house wins by simply making sure you keep playing.
6. 'Odds' and 'payout' are different things — True odds are your real chance. Payout is what they give you. The gap between them IS the house edge.
