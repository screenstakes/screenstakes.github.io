how roulette actually works before you ever bet

2. There is zero skill in the spin — Nothing you do changes where the ball lands. It's the purest luck game on the floor.
3. "Lucky" chips do nothing — Where you place them changes your odds, what color they are does not. It's all math.
4. Set a number and walk — Pick what you'll risk before you sit. Roulette is built to keep you "one more spin" forever.
5. The minimum bet is a trap too — Small bets feel safe, so you make more of them. The edge still adds up on every single one.
6. "Red is due after 5 blacks" — The wheel has no memory. Every spin is 50/50 again. The streak means nothing.
