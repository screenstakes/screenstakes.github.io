poker things I wish I knew before my first game night

1. Bet the same size every time — Big on good hands and small on bluffs is a dead giveaway. Keep it identical.
2. Watch the players, not your cards — Your hand doesn't change. Their behavior tells you whether to push or fold.
3. Position is power — Acting last means you've seen what everyone did first. Play more hands when you're last.
4. Don't slow-play monsters — Trapping usually just lets people fold. If you have the nuts, build the pot now.
5. Quit while you're up — The urge to keep going after a big win is exactly how you hand it all back.
6. You don't need to bluff much — New players bluff too often. Just bet when you have it and you'll win more than you think.
