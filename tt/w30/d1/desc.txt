the poker habits that actually make money

1. Watch the players, not your cards — Your hand doesn't change. Their behavior tells you whether to push or fold.
2. Position is power — Acting last means you've seen what everyone did first. Play more hands when you're last.
3. Don't slow-play monsters — Trapping usually just lets people fold. If you have the nuts, build the pot now.
4. Quit while you're up — The urge to keep going after a big win is exactly how you hand it all back.
5. You don't need to bluff much — New players bluff too often. Just bet when you have it and you'll win more than you think.
6. Learn hand rankings cold — Losing because you didn't know a flush beats a straight is the most avoidable way to go broke.
