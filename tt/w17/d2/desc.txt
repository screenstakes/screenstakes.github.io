the physical side of not getting read

1. Look at one fixed spot — Staring people down and avoiding eyes both read as information. Pick a neutral point on the felt and use it every hand.
2. Take the same time every decision — Snap-calling weak and tanking strong is the most common leak. Count to three on every action, even folds.
3. Sunglasses aren't the answer — They hide eyes and nothing else. Your hands, shoulders, and breathing give away far more than your pupils.
4. Practice folding the same way — Most players are careful when they're in a hand and sloppy when they fold. Your fold motion is data too.
5. Pick one resting position and keep it — Decide where your hands go before the cards come. Consistency beats acting — you can't leak what never changes.
6. Breathe out before you act — Big hands make people hold their breath. One slow exhale before every decision evens out your tells.
