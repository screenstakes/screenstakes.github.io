host poker night without the drama

1. Explain the rules before, not during — Nothing kills momentum like teaching mid-hand. Run one practice round first.
2. Have way more chips than you think — Running out of chips mid-game is the fastest way to kill the energy. Overbuy.
3. Set a hard end time — "One more hand" at 3am ruins the next one. End on a high note and they'll all come back.
4. Agree on the buy-in first — Set one number everyone antes and stick to it. Mismatched stacks end friendships at 1am.
5. Blinds keep it moving — Small forced bets each hand stop everyone from folding forever. The game needs pressure.
6. One dealer, passed each hand — Rotating who deals keeps it fair and gives everyone a turn in the best position.
