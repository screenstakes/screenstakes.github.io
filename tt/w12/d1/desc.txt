why slots are the hardest game to walk away from

1. Time flies on purpose — No clocks, no windows, free drinks. Every design choice exists to make you forget how long you've sat.
2. There's no "due" machine — A slot that hasn't paid in hours is exactly as likely to hit as one that just did. Pure random.
3. The near-miss is on purpose — Two 7s and a blank isn't bad luck. It's designed to feel like you almost won so you keep going.
4. Max bet doesn't change your odds — It only changes how fast you lose. The chance per spin is the same at any bet size.
5. They're loosest near the entrance — Casinos put higher-paying machines where crowds see winners. It's bait, and it works.
6. "Free spins" already cost you — The machine paid out less on every other spin to fund that bonus. Nothing there is free.
