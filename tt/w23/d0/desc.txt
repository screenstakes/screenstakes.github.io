6 poker tells that give your friends away

1. They reach for chips early — Grabbing chips before it's their turn is meant to scare you off. It usually means they're weak.
2. They suddenly sit up straight — Slouching then snapping to attention when the cards hit means their posture gave it away.
3. They start talking a lot — Chatty and friendly during a hand? People relax when they're strong and perform when they're lying.
4. They double-check their cards — Looking twice usually means they missed. Strong hands get remembered the first time.
5. They swallow hard — A dry gulp right after the cards come down is pure nerves. Big hand, and they know it.
6. They cover their mouth — Nobody hides their face when they have good cards. The hand comes up when the lie does.
