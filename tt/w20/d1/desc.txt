blackjack basics that save you money

1. Never copy the dealer — Standing on everything like they do ignores that you act first. It quietly bleeds your money.
2. Hit a soft 17 — An ace makes 17 safe to improve. Standing here throws away a free upgrade to a stronger hand.
3. Don't raise your bet after a loss — The next hand doesn't know you lost. Chasing it back is emotion, not strategy.
4. Short sessions only — The longer you sit, the more the edge grinds you down. Getting up early is your best defense.
5. Split, don't hit, on 8s vs weak cards — Turning your worst total into two fresh hands is the single biggest edge beginners skip.
6. Always split aces and 8s — Two 8s is 16, the worst hand in the game. Split them and get two real shots instead of one dead one.
