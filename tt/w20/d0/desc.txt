the unwritten rules of a good poker night

1. Have way more chips than you think — Running out of chips mid-game is the fastest way to kill the energy. Overbuy.
2. Set a hard end time — "One more hand" at 3am ruins the next one. End on a high note and they'll all come back.
3. Agree on the buy-in first — Set one number everyone antes and stick to it. Mismatched stacks end friendships at 1am.
4. Blinds keep it moving — Small forced bets each hand stop everyone from folding forever. The game needs pressure.
5. One dealer, passed each hand — Rotating who deals keeps it fair and gives everyone a turn in the best position.
6. No phones at the table — Half the game is reading faces. Phones kill the tells and the vibe at the same time.
