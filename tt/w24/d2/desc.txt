gambling 'strategies' that are complete nonsense

1. "I'm due for a win" — The gambler's fallacy. Cards, dice, and reels have zero memory. Losing 10 times in a row changes nothing about number 11.
2. "I have a lucky seat" — Every seat at the table has identical odds. The only thing your lucky chair changes is how long you sit in it.
3. "That machine is hot" — Every spin is independent. A machine that just paid is exactly as likely to pay again as one that hasn't in days.
4. "Betting more wins it back" — Chasing losses is the fastest way to turn a bad night into a disaster. The math doesn't care how much you need it.
5. "I can feel when it's coming" — Humans are pattern-matching machines in a system with no patterns. That feeling is your brain making things up.
6. "The dealer is against me" — Dealers have zero control over outcomes and usually want you winning. They're just the person turning the cards.
