the rules that make game night actually fun

1. Set a hard end time — "One more hand" at 3am ruins the next one. End on a high note and they'll all come back.
2. Agree on the buy-in first — Set one number everyone antes and stick to it. Mismatched stacks end friendships at 1am.
3. Blinds keep it moving — Small forced bets each hand stop everyone from folding forever. The game needs pressure.
4. One dealer, passed each hand — Rotating who deals keeps it fair and gives everyone a turn in the best position.
5. No phones at the table — Half the game is reading faces. Phones kill the tells and the vibe at the same time.
6. Cash out, don't add back — Endless rebuys turn a fun night into a money pit. Set a cutoff before you start.
