the discipline nobody talks about

1. Bank half the moment you double — Half goes in your pocket and never comes back out. Play the rest like it was always the plan.
2. Set an alarm, not a vibe — Time blindness is the whole design of the building. A phone alarm beats 'I'll know when.'
3. Never re-buy after cashing out — The cage is a finish line. Walking back to an ATM is how a winning night quietly becomes a losing one.
4. Tell someone your number — Saying 'I'm leaving at $200 up' out loud makes it real. Alone, you'll renegotiate with yourself.
5. Leave while it still feels good — The best exit feels slightly too early. If it feels like the right time, you already stayed too long.
6. Decide the exit before you enter — Pick a win number and a loss number at the door. Deciding while you're up is deciding while you're emotional.
