think at the table like a pro

3. The tightest player usually wins — The friend who folds a lot isn't boring. They're taking everyone's money by the end.
4. Don't chase every draw — Calling big bets hoping to hit your card is how chips leak all night. Do the quick math.
5. Pay attention when you're not in the hand — Folded early? Good. That's your free chance to study how everyone else plays.
6. Fold way more than you want to — The best players play fewer hands, not more. If it's trash, throw it away and wait.
