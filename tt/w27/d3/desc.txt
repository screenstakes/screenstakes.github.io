simple blackjack rules worth memorizing

1. Hit a soft 17 — An ace makes 17 safe to improve. Standing here throws away a free upgrade to a stronger hand.
2. Don't raise your bet after a loss — The next hand doesn't know you lost. Chasing it back is emotion, not strategy.
3. Short sessions only — The longer you sit, the more the edge grinds you down. Getting up early is your best defense.
4. Split, don't hit, on 8s vs weak cards — Turning your worst total into two fresh hands is the single biggest edge beginners skip.
5. Always split aces and 8s — Two 8s is 16, the worst hand in the game. Split them and get two real shots instead of one dead one.
6. Never split 10s — You already have 20. Splitting a winner to chase two maybes is how people go broke.
