the boring poker skills that actually work

1. Quit while you're up — The urge to keep going after a big win is exactly how you hand it all back.
2. You don't need to bluff much — New players bluff too often. Just bet when you have it and you'll win more than you think.
3. Learn hand rankings cold — Losing because you didn't know a flush beats a straight is the most avoidable way to go broke.
4. Only risk what you'd lose — The second the money matters, you play scared. Buy in small and play free.
5. The tightest player usually wins — The friend who folds a lot isn't boring. They're taking everyone's money by the end.
6. Don't chase every draw — Calling big bets hoping to hit your card is how chips leak all night. Do the quick math.
