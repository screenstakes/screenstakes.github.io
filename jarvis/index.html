JARVIS — a personal assistant service

JARVIS is a private, AI-powered personal assistant operated by Michael Fitzgerald, a sole proprietor based in Texas, United States.

JARVIS answers phone calls and sends text messages for its owner. It helps with everyday personal organization: calendar events, homework and appointment reminders, school assignment alerts, and short informational updates that the account holder has asked for.

What JARVIS does

  • Adds events to the owner's calendar and confirms them by text
  • Sends one-off reminders the owner scheduled ("your exam is tomorrow at 8 AM")
  • Sends school assignment and schedule alerts the owner requested
  • Replies to text messages the owner sends it

Who receives messages

Messages are sent to the account holder himself and, occasionally, to a small set of personal contacts (family and friends) — and only when they have opted in, or when the account holder explicitly asks JARVIS to relay a specific one-time message. This is a personal service, not a marketing program. No advertising or promotional messages are ever sent.
