SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: July 28, 2026

1. Program description

JARVIS is a personal assistant text messaging program operated by Michael Fitzgerald, a sole proprietor based in Texas, United States (the "Program"). The Program sends personal, non-commercial messages: reminders, calendar event confirmations, school assignment and schedule alerts, and conversational replies from the assistant. Messages are sent from +1 (972) 432-5446. The Program never sends marketing, advertising, or promotional content.

2. Consent and opt-in

You can opt in to the Program by texting the keyword START to +1 (972) 432-5446, or by initiating a conversation with the assistant. A one-time message may also be relayed to a personal contact at the account holder's explicit direction; such messages identify the Program and include opt-out instructions. Consent is not a condition of purchasing anything. Full details: Text Message Program.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on use — typically 0–5 messages per day.

4. Fees

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. The Program itself charges nothing.

5. Opting out

Text STOP to any Program message to cancel at any time. You will receive one final message confirming your opt-out, and no further messages will be sent. You can re-join at any time by texting START.

6. Help and support

Text HELP to any Program message, or email m.fitz36@icloud.com for assistance.

7. Supported carriers and liability

The Program is available on major U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Message delivery is subject to effective transmission by your mobile carrier.

8. Privacy

Our Privacy Policy describes how information is handled. We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

9. Changes

We may update these terms; the current version will always be posted at this page with an updated date.
