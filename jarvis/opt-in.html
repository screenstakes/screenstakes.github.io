JARVIS Text Message Program

How to opt in, what you'll receive, and how to stop.

To opt in: text START to +1 (972) 432-5446

By texting START to +1 (972) 432-5446 you agree to receive personal assistant text messages (reminders, calendar confirmations, schedule alerts, and conversational replies) from JARVIS, the personal assistant service operated by Michael Fitzgerald. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help and STOP to cancel at any time. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Ways you can opt in

  • Text a keyword: text START to +1 (972) 432-5446, as shown above.
  • Start a conversation: the account holder opts in by texting or calling his own assistant at the same number; replying to JARVIS is itself the opt-in for that conversation.
  • Verbal request: a family member or friend may ask the account holder in person to have JARVIS send them a message; the first message they receive confirms the program and how to opt out, and one-time relays are only ever sent at the account holder's explicit direction.

What you'll receive

Personal, non-commercial messages only: reminders you or the account holder set, calendar confirmations, school assignment alerts, and short replies from the assistant. No marketing, no advertising, no promotions — ever.

Frequency, rates, and support

  • Message frequency varies based on use (typically 0–5 messages per day).
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
  • Reply HELP for help at any time, or email m.fitz36@icloud.com.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. You'll receive one final confirmation message and nothing further.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Mobile opt-in information is never shared with or sold to third parties — see the Privacy Policy.
