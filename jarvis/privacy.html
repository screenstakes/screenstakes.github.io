Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how JARVIS — a personal assistant service operated by Michael Fitzgerald, a sole proprietor based in Texas, United States ("we," "us," or "our") — collects, uses, and protects information in connection with its text messaging program at +1 (972) 432-5446.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone numbers of the account holder and of individuals who opt in to receive messages.
  • Message content exchanged with the assistant, used solely to generate replies and carry out requested tasks (for example, setting a reminder).
  • Opt-in and opt-out records (keywords such as START, STOP, and HELP, with timestamps), kept to honor consent choices.

How we use information

Information is used only to operate the personal assistant service: delivering reminders, calendar confirmations, schedule alerts, and conversational replies that the recipient signed up for; honoring opt-outs; and maintaining the security of the service. We do not use this information for advertising or marketing of any kind.

Sharing — we do not sell or share your mobile information

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except as required to deliver messages through our telecommunications service providers (for example, Twilio Inc., which transmits the messages), or as required by law.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies based on use (typically 0–5 messages per day). Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time; you will receive a single confirmation message and nothing further. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at m.fitz36@icloud.com.

Data retention and security

Records are retained only as long as needed to operate the service and honor consent choices, and are stored on private, access-controlled systems. You may request deletion of your information at any time using the contact above.

Children's privacy

This is a private, invitation-based personal service. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
