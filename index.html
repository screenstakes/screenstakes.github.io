How it works

Your minutes become the chips.

1

Set your stakes

Pick the apps that eat your day — Instagram, TikTok, whatever it is for you. Each morning you get a fresh bankroll of minutes for them. That's your real allowance, enforced by iPhone's own Screen Time.

2

Play for keeps

Blackjack, roulette, slots and poker against the house — but the chips are your minutes. Win, and you've earned more time in your apps. Lose, and the scroll gets shorter today.

3

The locks are real

When a bankroll hits zero, those apps actually lock — at the system level, even with Screen Stakes closed. Not a guilt trip. Not a pop-up you swipe away. Locked, until you win time back or the clock resets.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Is this gambling?

No — and the difference isn't small print. Gambling risks money. Screen Stakes has none: nothing to deposit, buy, or cash out, and minutes can never convert into anything of value. What you're playing for is whether your own afternoon belongs to you or your feed.

How do the locks actually work?

Screen Stakes is built on the same Screen Time framework iPhone parents' controls use. When a bankroll runs out, iOS itself shields those apps — system-level, even with Screen Stakes closed. Winning time back lifts the lock instantly.

What if I really need my phone?

Essentials never lock. You choose an always-allowed list (messages, maps, banking, anything you'd need in a pinch) that no game result can ever touch — and a protected block of minutes can never be staked, so your day never hits zero.

What data do you collect?

None. There are no accounts and no server. Your balances, streaks, and screen-time data live on your phone and never leave it. Read the privacy policy — it's short, because there's nothing to disclose.
